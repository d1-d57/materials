# Category Theory Course Materials (2025–26)

## Official Course Resources

**Lecture Notes:**
- "Lecture Notes by Andrew Pitts and Marcelo Fiore" available through the VLE

**Exercise Sheets:**
- Sheets 1–5 available on the VLE platform
- One-hour example classes provided for exercise support
- Discussion forum available for advance questions

## Recommended Textbooks

1. **Mac Lane, Saunders.** _Categories for the Working Mathematician._ Graduate Texts in Mathematics 5 (Second Ed.). Springer, 1988.

2. **Leinster, Tom.** _Basic Category Theory_ (CUP, 2014) — available as a free PDF on arXiv

## Supplementary Learning Resources

- **Logic Matters** guide to online category theory materials
- **nLab** category theory entries
- **The Catsters' video series** on category theory
- **Bartosz Milewski's** _Category Theory for Programmers_ (GitHub repository)
- **Applied Category Theory** website
- **Marcelo Fiore's** _Rough notes on presheaves_ (PDF)

All materials are accessible through the course page or linked repositories.
