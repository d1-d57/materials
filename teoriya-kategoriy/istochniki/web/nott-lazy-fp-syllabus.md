## Course Overview

**Instructor:** Thorsten Altenkirch (University of Nottingham)

**Program:** Basic course for MGS 2025

The course is an introduction to category theory emphasizing applications in computer science, especially functional programming, from a type theoretic perspective.

### Topics Covered

- Categories
- Duality
- Functors and natural transformations
- Adjunctions
- The Yoneda lemma
- Products and coproducts
- Cartesian closed categories
- Initial algebras and terminal coalgebras
- Limits and colimits
- Monads and comonads
