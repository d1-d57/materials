# Category Theory Course – University of Cambridge (2025–26)

## Course Overview
This Part II Computer Science course introduces foundational concepts in category theory and their applications to logic and programming language semantics. The course is taught by Prof Marcelo Fiore during Michaelmas term.

**Key Details:**
- Code: CAT
- Duration: 16 hours (8 lectures, 2 hours each)
- Format: In-person lectures
- Class limit: 15 students maximum
- Prerequisites: Discrete Mathematics, Foundations of Computer Science, Semantics of Programming Languages

## Course Aims
The course demonstrates how "category theory provides a unified treatment of mathematical properties" applicable across algebra, geometry, computer science, logic, and linguistics. Students explore how categories enable comparison between mathematical branches and problem transfer across disciplines.

## Syllabus Topics
Key content includes:

- Categorical fundamentals: definitions, sets/functions, commutative diagrams
- Structural concepts: isomorphisms, terminal/initial objects, products/coproducts
- Cartesian closed categories and exponential objects
- Intuitionistic Propositional Logic semantics
- Simply Typed Lambda Calculus and the Curry-Howard-Lawvere correspondence
- Functors, natural transformations, and adjunctions
- Dependent types and category equivalence
- Presheaves and the Yoneda Lemma
- Monads and computational lambda calculus

## Assessment
- Graded exercise sheet: 25%
- Take-home test: 75%
