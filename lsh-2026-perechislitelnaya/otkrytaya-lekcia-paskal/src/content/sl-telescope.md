{@2 .eq}$|A_5|+|A_6|+\dots=\Bigl[\binom{10}5-\binom{10}6\Bigr]+\Bigl[\binom{10}6-\binom{10}7\Bigr]+\dots$

{@3 .eq}$\dots+\Bigl[\binom{10}9-\binom{10}{10}\Bigr]+\Bigl[\binom{10}{10}-\binom{10}{11}\Bigr]$
